Habit Recovery – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

Habit Recovery is a simple Android app for building habits gently and restarting after missed days. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

Your habit data stays on your device

Your habits, schedules, goals, reminder times and check-ins are saved only in a database on your phone. They are never uploaded to any server, and there is no account or sign-up. Uninstalling the app deletes all of this data.

What the app does not do

  • No account, login, analytics or tracking of your habits.
  • No social features and no sharing of your data with anyone.
  • No access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone or files.

Reminders

If you turn reminders on, the app asks for notification permission. Reminders are scheduled by your phone itself; nothing is sent from a server.

Advertising

The app is free and shows one small banner ad provided by Google AdMob. To show it, Google may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address and general device information, as described in Google's advertising policies. Where the law requires (for example in the European Economic Area and the UK), the app first asks for your consent through Google's consent form, and you can review or change your choice later in Settings > Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Android settings. Your habit data is never shared with advertisers.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13.
